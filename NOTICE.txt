Text sources and licensing

Sanskrit verse text: the Bhagavad Gita, from the Mahabharata. Ancient
Sanskrit text, in the public domain.

English translation: Annie Wood Besant's 1922 translation ("The Lord's
Song"), 4th edition, sourced from Wikisource (Bhagavad-Gita_(Besant_4th)).
Published 1922, in the public domain in the United States.

Chapter titles use the traditional Sanskrit discourse names (ancient,
public domain). Chapter context summaries (2-4 sentences each) were
written originally for this app, not copied from any existing app or
translator's commentary.

All 701 verses and 18 chapter summaries are bundled with this app and
read entirely from local storage on the device; there is no server and
no account.

"Shloka" is this app's own name. It is not affiliated with, and does not
reuse branding, UI, or code from, any other Bhagavad Gita or scripture
app.
